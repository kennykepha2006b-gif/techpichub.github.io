Affiliate disclosure

How we may earn revenue and how we intend to keep recommendations clear.

Current status: TechPick Hub has not claimed approval by any affiliate network. Current product links are ordinary links to official manufacturer websites.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is tracked by a merchant or network. If a reader clicks and completes a qualifying action, the publisher may receive a commission at no extra cost to the reader.

How we will disclose links

If we join a program and add affiliate links, we will disclose the relationship clearly near relevant recommendations and on this page, following applicable network and market rules.

Does commission guarantee a recommendation?

No. We aim to explain intended use, specifications, limitations and trade-offs. We will not claim to have tested a product unless we have actually done so.

Prices and availability

Prices, stock, product details, warranty and shipping may change. Verify the seller's current listing and terms before purchasing.
