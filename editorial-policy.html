Our standards

Research first. Explain clearly. Correct mistakes.

Our intended standards for educational articles and future product comparisons.

Research sources

For product facts, we intend to prioritize manufacturer specifications, manuals and warranty documentation, supplemented by reputable independent sources when useful. We will note when details vary by configuration.

How we compare

We compare relevant factors such as intended use, price, performance, compatibility, upgradeability, battery life, warranty and local availability. The right criteria depend on the product and reader.

Testing and experience

We distinguish research-based explainers from hands-on reviews. We will not say “we tested” or publish original benchmark claims unless the work was actually performed and can be described honestly.

Commercial relationships

Affiliate approval or a commission is not proof that a product is best. We will disclose affiliate links and will not invent traffic, audience statistics, sales results, tests or advertiser relationships.

Corrections

Technology changes. We intend to review important facts periodically and assess reader corrections. Contact us through the contact page.

What readers should verify

  • Exact model and configuration.
  • Current price, shipping and stock.
  • Warranty, return terms and seller reputation.
  • Compatibility and upgrade limits.
This describes our intended standard; it is not a claim that every product has been independently tested.
