Who we are

Technology should be easier to understand.

TechPick Hub is an educational technology website focused on laptops, PC components, storage, memory and everyday computing.

Our mission

Buying technology can be confusing. Our goal is to explain technical details in plain language and provide practical checklists readers can use before spending money.

Who we serve

We create resources for students, first-time buyers, home users, creators and people upgrading a computer. We aim to explain local availability, warranty, import costs and value where relevant.

What readers can expect

  • Plain-language explanations of specifications.
  • Checklists for comparing exact configurations.
  • Clear notes where prices and availability vary.
  • Transparent disclosure of commercial relationships.
  • Corrections when we find errors.

Funding and independence

Current links are ordinary official-manufacturer links and are not affiliate tracking links. If we join an affiliate program, we may earn commissions from qualifying purchases through clearly disclosed links. A commission does not increase the buyer's price. We will not claim product testing unless it actually occurred.

A polished site cannot guarantee affiliate approval. Networks make their own decisions based on their policies and review criteria.
